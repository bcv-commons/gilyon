# Collections hosted on gilyon.org

Collections published at `https://gilyon.org/collections/<name>/`. Most
collections in the catalogue are hosted by their publishers and are not in
this folder (`docs/catalogue.md`).

The licence of each collection is stated in its `manifest.json`. The
licence of this repository (`LICENSE`) does not apply to them.

| Folder | Content | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `swe_svk/` | Anchors of the inline notes of Svenska Kärnbibeln (The Swedish Core Bible) on Joshua: 292 positions among the Hebrew words, computed by software and not reviewed. Anchors only: the wording of the notes is not included. | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, Svenska Kärnbibeln (SKB), karnbibeln.se |

The folder names `c`, `lang` and `publisher` are taken by the catalogue.
