# Collections

The catalogue of annotation collections published in Gilyon.

It has one entry so far, hosted on this site:

| Collection | Content | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [The Swedish Core Bible: anchors of the notes on Joshua](swe_svk/manifest.json) | 292 positions among the Hebrew words, one for each inline note of Svenska Kärnbibeln on Joshua. Computed by software from a word alignment of the Swedish text; not reviewed. Anchors only: the wording of the notes is not included. | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, Svenska Kärnbibeln (SKB), [karnbibeln.se](https://karnbibeln.se) |

The catalogue points to collections where their publishers host them, and to
translations of them published by others. It holds no annotations and no
note text. The machine-readable catalogue starts at
[`index.json`](index.json), which names one file per language and per
publisher; its format is section 16.4 of the
[specification](../../spec/gilyon-0.1.md).

How to get a collection or a translation listed, and what is verified:
[listing and verification](../../docs/catalogue.md).
